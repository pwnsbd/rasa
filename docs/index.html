Rasa

Pour a painting's style onto your photos

Drop in any reference image and Rasa distills its look into an Essence: brushwork, palette, texture. Drag that Essence onto your own photo and watch it transform, while the scene keeps its shape. Everything runs on your own GPU.

How it works

No prompts, no sliders full of jargon. Three moves.

Distill

Drop a painting, print or texture into the Distillation Room. Rasa extracts its style into a reusable Essence bottle.

Pour

Pick a photo and drag the bottle onto it. Live progress shows each step; a restyle takes about 15 seconds on a laptop GPU.

Keep

Every creation lands in your Media page, ready to save, compare, or turn into a relit animation.

Under the hood

A desktop app with a local Python engine. Your photos never leave your computer.

The pipeline

  • SDXL image-to-image with InstantStyle (IP-Adapter), which carries style while leaving layout alone
  • Tile ControlNet keeps your photo's structure: faces, buildings, horizon
  • Multi-crop "purification" so an Essence captures style, not the reference's subject
  • Adapts to your GPU: keeps models resident when memory allows, offloads when it doesn't
~15sper restyle, RTX 5070 Ti laptop

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • NVIDIA GPU; tested on 12 GB VRAM. Smaller cards run in a slower low-memory mode
  • 16 GB RAM minimum, 32 GB recommended
  • ~15 GB free disk; models (~11.5 GB) download once on first launch
  • The installer isn't code-signed yet. If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose More info → Run anyway
